03 / READ THE RESULT

Know what a
“wheel found” means.

The checker asks whether any release has any wheel. It does not prove a compatible wheel exists for your Python version, platform, architecture, or chosen release.

Use its report to investigate. Let your installer enforce --only-binary :all: for the final dependency set. The checker is not a malware scanner.
